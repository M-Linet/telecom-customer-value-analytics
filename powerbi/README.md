# Power BI: Jamii Mobile customer value & retention dashboard

> The dashboard is a **Power BI project** (`JamiiMobile.pbip` plus the `JamiiMobile.SemanticModel/` and `JamiiMobile.Report/` folders), stored as text (TMDL model, PBIR report) so that Git shows meaningful diffs. Both parts are generated by `scripts/build_pbip.py`. The model was loaded and refreshed in Power BI Desktop (Nov 2026 build), and the KPI cards were checked against `kpi_reconciliation.csv`.
>
> **To open it:**
> 1. Run `python -m src.export_powerbi` to write the CSV extract to `data/processed/powerbi/`.
> 2. Run `python scripts/build_pbip.py` to point the `DataFolder` parameter at your copy of the project.
> 3. Open `JamiiMobile.pbip` and click **Refresh**.
>
> To get a single file, use *File → Save as → .pbix*. All data is synthetic, for a fictional operator.

## 1. Data model
```
                    dim_date (date, marked as date table)
                  /        |          \            \
 fact_customer_month  fact_service_revenue  fact_complaints  fact_campaigns
                  \        |          /            /
                    dim_customer (customer_id)

 cohort_retention        (cohort × months since acquisition, from SQL q11; no relationships)
 complaint_churn_link    (value tier × complaint outcome, from SQL q18; no relationships)
```
| Table | Grain | Rows |
|---|---|---|
| dim_date | day, 1 Jan 2024 – 31 Oct 2026 | 1,035 |
| dim_customer | customer (segment, engagement status, county, plan, cohort) | 28,496 |
| fact_customer_month | customer × subscribed month (the SQL grid `telco.mv_customer_month`) | 602,836 |
| fact_service_revenue | customer × month × service | about 1.2M |
| fact_complaints | complaint | 10,559 |
| fact_campaigns | campaign contact | 35,523 |

Decisions:
- **Every customer measure reads from `fact_customer_month`**, the same grid the SQL and notebooks use, so the opening base, active customers and churn have one definition.
- **Single-direction relationships** from the dimensions to the facts. There are no fact-to-fact paths.
- **The cohort heatmap and the complaint–churn chart use small tables pre-aggregated in SQL.** Cohort retention needs a fixed per-cohort denominator, and the complaint–churn link needs a fixed population and time window. Both are clearer and safer in SQL than in DAX. These two visuals therefore do not react to the slicers, and their titles say what population they cover.
- Foreign keys and helper columns are hidden. `month_short` is sorted by `month_number`, and `tenure_band` by `tenure_band_order`.

## 2. Measures (44, in `_Measures`)
| Measure | DAX (short form) | Definition |
|---|---|---|
| Revenue | `SUM(fact_customer_month[revenue])` | billed purchases, reversals excluded |
| Active Customer Months | rows with `is_active = TRUE` | customer-months with any purchase or usage |
| ARPU | `DIVIDE([Revenue], [Active Customer Months])` | revenue per active customer per month |
| Opening Base | rows with `in_opening_base = TRUE` | lines open on day 1 of the month |
| Churned Customers | rows with `is_churn_month = TRUE` | lines closed in the month |
| Churn Rate % | `DIVIDE([Churned Customers], [Opening Base])` | monthly churn |
| Avg Monthly Churn % (from May 2024) | Churn Rate % with `KEEPFILTERS(dim_date[date] >= 1 May 2024)` | excludes months before the first possible closure |
| Annualised Churn % | `1 - POWER(1 - [Avg Monthly Churn %], 12)` | compounding |
| … (latest month) | `VAR m = MAX(month) RETURN CALCULATE(…, month = m)` | the cards show the last month in the selection |
| Revenue Share / Customer Share of Selection | `DIVIDE(x, CALCULATE(x, ALLSELECTED(dim_customer)))` | share within the current slicer selection |
| Retention % | `AVERAGE(cohort_retention[retention_pct]) / 100` | cohort heatmap value |
| Retention Colour / Font Colour | `SWITCH` on retention bands | field-value conditional formatting for the heatmap (one blue ramp) |
| Declining Customers / Revenue at Risk (annualised) | filtered on engagement status; (May–Jul − Aug–Oct revenue) ÷ 3 × 12 | early-warning list |
| Complaints per 1,000 Subscriber-Months | `DIVIDE([Complaints], COUNTROWS(fact_customer_month)) * 1000` | exposure-adjusted complaint rate |
| Avg Days to Resolve / Unresolved Complaint % | resolved tickets only / status ≠ Resolved | service quality |
| Campaign Acceptance % | Accepted ÷ contacts | campaign response |
| Δ Revenue / Δ Active Customers / Δ ARPU | first vs last month in the selection, as `▲ 12%  Jan 24 → Oct 26` text | tile subtitles |
| Monthly Churn Text / Risk Share Text / County Status | formatted text; County Status = `▲ Watch` at 25+ complaints per 1,000 | tile subtitles and status column |
| Colour County Status / Segment Churn / Complaint Category / Plan Churn | hex colour per data point | highlight rules (orange = the problem) |

## 3. Pages
Canvas 1280 × 720. The masthead on every page carries the page tabs and three slicers: **Date**, **Type** (prepaid/postpaid) and **Segment**.

| Page | Headline | Visuals |
|---|---|---|
| Overview | Revenue up 19% a year, but 1 in 6 customers still leaves | monthly revenue with the July 2025 price change marked, revenue by service, active customers; 4 tiles; "What we'd do" |
| Segmentation | The top 10% of customers bring 36% of revenue | share of customers vs share of revenue by segment, ARPU by tenure, revenue by county; 4 tiles |
| Retention | Customers leave in months 4–6, cheap prepaid first | cohort retention heatmap, monthly churn with the price rise marked, churn by segment (Bronze highlighted), declining-customer watch list; 2 tiles |
| Experience | Unresolved complaints are where customers are lost | churn by complaint outcome within value tiers, complaints by category, days to resolve, network status by county; 1 tile |

## 5. Design and accessibility
The layout is editorial with a network-operations feel: each page reads like a page of a business newspaper printed for a control room.
- **Story first:** a kicker, a headline that states the finding (Georgia serif) and a two-line standfirst, then one hero chart and two supporting charts.
- **Right column:** big-number tiles with ▲/▼ deltas, then a **"What we'd do"** box that turns the page into a recommendation.
- **Control-room theme:** dark canvas, teal accent, a signal-bar strip under the masthead (`signal_strip.png`) and a `▲ Watch` / `● Normal` network status table.
- **Notes on the charts:** dashed reference lines mark the July 2025 bundle price rise on the revenue and churn trends.
- **Colour has one job per chart:** blue = focus, orange = the problem, grey = context. Highlight rules are measures (`Colour …`).
- **Colour-blind safe:** on the dark panel (`#1b2027`), blue `#3987e5` and orange `#d95926` pass the palette validator: worst-case separation under simulated protanopia/deuteranopia/tritanopia is ΔE 26.8 (target ≥ 8), with at least 3:1 contrast. There is no red/green pairing. Colour is never the only cue: bars carry data labels, subtitles name what the colours mean, status uses text plus symbol, and deltas use arrows.
- The style lives in `scripts/pbi_editorial.py`, shared with the other portfolio dashboards.
